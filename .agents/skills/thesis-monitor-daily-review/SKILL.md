# Thesis Monitor Daily Review

Invoke this workflow as `$thesis-monitor-daily-review` from each scheduled task.

Use this skill only for immutable packets under `data/ai_review/inbox`. Do not browse the web, call an external API, modify application code, or alter the database, official assessment, or Telegram delivery. In the delivery pilot, only the local validator/dispatcher may release the validated result; the Codex analyst still writes only its claim-specific JSON.

## Workflow

1. Claim one packet for the requested market:

   ```bash
   .venv/bin/python -m app.jobs.ai_review claim --market us --owner <task-name>
   ```

   Use `--market kr` for a Korean close review. The US primary task uses `--wait-seconds 300 --poll-seconds 15 --lease-minutes 10` so it can wait briefly for the 08:05-08:20 backend readiness gate while still allowing the 08:30 backup to reclaim an interrupted claim. Other workers use the default lease unless their scheduled prompt says otherwise. If the result is `no_pending_packet`, stop without modifying anything.

2. Read the returned `packet_path`, this skill, [daily-review-policy.md](references/daily-review-policy.md), [knowledge-index.md](references/knowledge-index.md), [chart-knowledge-index.md](references/chart-knowledge-index.md), and [output-schema.json](references/output-schema.json). Follow each stock's `knowledge_routing` against [Investment Knowledge v3](references/investment-thesis-analysis-monitoring-knowledge.md). When `chart_knowledge_routing.available=true`, also follow that route against [Chart Knowledge v1](references/stock-chart-value-analysis-knowledge-v1.md). Do not replace either routed source with general model knowledge.

3. Review the market once and every stock in `stocks`. For the market, begin with `key_change_fact_ids`, connect index, sector, rates, FX, commodities, or risk facts into a supported market structure, and use only `transmission_candidates` to connect those facts to `portfolio_exposure_groups`. Obey `macro_temporal_eligibility`: only `CURRENT_OBSERVATION` facts may create a current daily signal or ticker transmission; `PRIOR_MARKET_SESSION` facts require explicit prior-session wording; `REFERENCE_LAGGING`, `STALE_FOR_DAILY_SIGNAL`, and `UNAVAILABLE` facts cannot be presented as today's change. Breadth and market-wide flows remain Unknown when coverage says unavailable. Use only `fact_id` values from each `fact_catalog` in `facts_used`. For a US packet, consume every `us_market_digest_plan.items[]` entry whose `required_consumption=true`: preserve its bounded `claim_text`, cite all `evidence_refs` in the owning market section and `facts_used`, and do not silently replace CURRENT_MARKET, PARTICIPATION_STYLE/RSP, or SECTOR_DISPERSION with an unrelated relative-performance fact. Every `required_market_fact_ids` entry must appear in `facts_used`, in `interpretation`, and in an `important_changes` item. For a US packet, these are the fresh KOSPI200/KOSDAQ150 night-futures facts selected by the backend. Interpret their relationship as Korean opening-price context, never as company-thesis confirmation. Record only framework names routed for the market in `frameworks_used`; never copy a stock industry framework such as `hyperscaler_capex_transmission` into the market owner. Treat absent information as unknown. Investment Knowledge v3 and backend facts take precedence over Chart Knowledge examples or valuation shortcuts.

   Before drafting stock prose, make one portfolio-level selection pass. For each stock, read `runtime_specificity_plan`, `industry_reasoning_plan`, and `current_price_context`, then choose one primary decision point, one supporting fact relationship, one material Unknown, and one next confirmation. The plan lists candidates, not conclusions: rank them by the stock's verified decision materiality. Respect every candidate `owner`: ADR/depositary statements belong only to `security_identity`, industry causality belongs to `industry_driver`, and support/resistance/RR belong to `price_context`. `framework_roles.investment_industry` and `framework_roles.price_context` are separate audit domains. `chart_risk_reward` may appear in `frameworks_used` only when the packet explicitly lists it under the price-context role; it is never an industry or valuation framework. Do not let a shared safety method, a generic cash-flow checklist, or the mere presence of a supply delta become the primary point across the portfolio. If several stocks genuinely share the same conclusion, ground each one in its own current facts and industry driver rather than manufacturing surface variation.

   Apply the packet's `security_reasoning_policy` before creating prose. A domestic common or `verified_non_depositary` security cannot produce an ADR, ADS, depositary-ratio, or ordinary-per-ADR reasoning candidate. `unknown` or `conflict` may support only a neutral security-basis caution; it cannot assert a verified security type or ratio. A verified depositary identity may use depositary language, but ratio language additionally requires `depositary_ratio_reasoning_allowed=true`. Suppressed candidates stay audit-only and must not be paraphrased back into another section.

4. Fill the schema-4 reasoning sections. Each section must contain concise `text` and supporting `fact_ids`. Market `portfolio_transmission` also requires an exact backend `portfolio_group`, and each item must cite a fact allowed for that group. `next_checks` must cite the next available backend market fact that could change the interpretation; a generic or fact-free check is invalid. Keep no more than four important changes, four relevant portfolio groups, three next checks, and three material cautions. Keep operational method notes and session provenance in the audit packet, not in user-facing market prose. For every investment-related number, put a draft-only placeholder such as `{{numeric:price_now}}` in the exact prose field and add one `numeric_fact_refs` item to that market or stock review with `ref_id`, `fact_id`, `field_path`, and `text_ref`. When Korean grammar needs a particle, set the typed `postposition` family (`은/는`, `이/가`, `을/를`, or `와/과`) on that reference and leave the raw particle out of prose; the binder resolves it from the canonical formatted unit. `role` is mandatory and exact for every zone endpoint: `lower` for `zone_low` and `upper` for `zone_high`; omit it for a single pivot or non-zone value. Keep `numeric_claims` empty for these references. The backend resolves the canonical value, unit, semantic, source-aware label, formatter, user text, and final schema-4 `numeric_claims`, then removes `numeric_fact_refs` before schema validation. Use only registry rows with `registered=true`, `prose_allowed=true`, and compatible scope. Do not type the raw number in prose or calculate a new value.
   For a directional working-capital relation, bind `fields.gap_percentage_points_signed` and write the exact packet comparator and direction around the placeholder, for example `재고 증가율은 매출원가 증가율을 {{numeric:inventory_gap}} 밑돌았습니다`. The relation ID must appear once in `facts_used` and in `business_earnings.fact_ids`, and its one numeric reference must be owned by `business_earnings.text`. The signed relation owns `higher/lower/above/below`; `fields.gap_percentage_points_abs` is allowed only for a non-directional absolute difference and cannot support directional wording. Preserve the relation's lhs, rhs, comparison basis, period, and relation ID exactly; never use an Inventory-vs-COGS relation to describe Revenue.

5. When safe anchors are available, use 2-4 decisive numbers in `core_judgment`, use only the packet's actual business/earnings Fact candidates in `business_earnings`, use current price plus the active dynamic level and provided risk/reward in `price_positioning`, and use at least two relevant multiples or relative metrics in `valuation_analysis`. `business_earnings_policy.minimum_numeric_anchor_count=0`: there is no numeric quota for that section. A `valuation:*` TTM EPS or BVPS remains valuation or balance-sheet context and cannot be copied into business prose merely to fill the section; an earnings-period EPS may be used only when it is itself material earnings evidence. When the policy has no business Fact candidate, keep the section numeric-free and state the routed company's specific missing driver. Every financial amount or directly attached growth/margin claim must use the backend's field-specific verified amount-period label. A filing period is not an amount period: one filing may contain both a single-quarter amount and a cumulative amount, and an amount whose exact source row, consolidated/separate basis, amount period, or comparison period is unknown is not prose eligible. For KR only, bind every eligible foreign/institution 1-day, 5-day, and 20-day fact in `supply_analysis`; a directional actor/horizon statement without its exact canonical numeric claim is invalid, and a missing cell remains a specific Unknown. For US, use only verified volume, relative-volume, or explicitly supplied positioning facts; generic `수급` language and the KR investor-horizon frame are invalid without an explicit US flow fact. Use `monitoring_state.current`, `previous`, and `delta` to explain what changed since the previous final assessment. A registered price rule is primary only while its lifecycle and relevance say it is current; after a confirmation is crossed, move to hold/retest and the next verified dynamic support/resistance. This is a validation boundary, not optional style: when a market or stock has at least four registered, prose-allowed numeric anchors, submitting zero numeric claims is a hard failure. Fresh required night-futures facts each require an exact market numeric claim. Missing data never authorizes invention. Explain why missing OCF, capex, FCF, inventory, or ROIC would change the judgment instead of merely saying that cash generation needs checking.
   When `cash_flow_user_visible.ai_enabled=true`, include its exact `required_period_label` in `business_earnings.text` and keep the supplied fiscal identity unchanged. Follow `duration_basis`, `allowed_period_claims`, and `forbidden_period_claims`: never shorten YTD to a standalone quarter, convert a fiscal period to a calendar period, or annualize it. The selected number is backend PPE-only FCF under `fcf_scope`, not management-defined FCF. For price prose, declare only IDs in `runtime_specificity_plan.price_fact_ownership.allowed_fact_ids`; an ID listed in `unavailable_fact_ids` must not appear in any section. In particular, when `current_rr_fact_id` is null, describe the supplied blocking reason without inventing the canonical RR Fact ID.
   `Anchor` is internal analysis terminology. Do not expose `앵커` in user-facing Korean prose. Write `최근 확인된 핵심 실적`, `현재 평가 기준`, or `핵심 숫자` according to context.
   Select at least one company-specific investment question instead of filling a shared stock template. Before prose, assign each selected item an owner, evidence type, decision role, section, and specificity key. If the same generic candidate would recur across three or more stocks, suppress it unless each occurrence has different subject-specific evidence and a different decision consequence. Do not seek credit by swapping synonyms. When two relevant valuation facts exist, compare their direction and explain the expectation or risk they imply under the routed industry framework; do not calculate a new spread or call a modeled value consensus. Keep the most material one or two Unknowns and next checks rather than forcing an identical checklist across all stocks. New-observer and holder text must not normalize to the same sentence: the former states what would make new capital's price/fact asymmetry acceptable, while the latter separates the current chart review boundary from fundamental thesis deterioration. Neither is a trading command.
   A sentence made only of two or more numeric placeholders separated by semicolons is not analysis. Fold the numeric evidence into the same sentence as that stock's subject-specific decision predicate. For selected cash flow, use `rendered_fallback_text` as the factual period/scope/industry seed, then keep or improve its company mechanism without reducing it to a portfolio-wide `period + FCF number` template.
   Suppress user-visible methodology that the backend already enforces. Do not repeat phrases such as `과거 가격 규칙을 동적 지지로 승격하지 않습니다`, `수급을 사업 논리와 분리합니다`, or a generic `OCF/CAPEX/FCF를 확인해야 합니다` across stocks. Instead, state the current lifecycle, the exact actor and horizon relationship, or the routed industry's one or two missing drivers. Do not open multiple stock sections with `현재 판단의 핵심 숫자는 ...` or `현재 확인된 핵심 숫자는 ...`; connect the selected facts directly into that company's decision question.
   Apply `risk_reward_delta_policy` before drafting a prior/current RR comparison. A plain numeric improvement or deterioration is not material by itself. When `decision_candidate_allowed=false`, omit the previous/current pair and use the current price structure already owned by `price_positioning`. When it is true, the pair may appear only as part of the selected primary price transition and must connect the canonical lifecycle or structure change to that stock's entry decision; do not emit a standalone `이전 RR; 현재 RR` sentence in `supply_analysis`. RR availability and formulas remain backend-owned.
   Keep `priority_watch` and `next_checks` semantically distinct. A watch item is an ongoing thesis driver or risk; it must not be the next filing, earnings release, or official-data event. A next check is the event-oriented confirmation that could validate or change the current judgment. Do not copy the same bullet into both lists. If either list adds no distinct decision information, leave it empty and let adaptive rendering suppress the section.
   Give each exact numeric fact one primary owner section. The exact `current_price_risk_reward_ratio` has one occurrence and one primary field: `price_positioning.text`. State only its supported entry meaning in `core_judgment`, observer, holder, supply, and next-check prose; do not bind the same current RR there. A canonical material transition may compare previous/current RR inside the primary price transition, but it does not create a second current-RR owner. Other decision-critical facts may appear twice only when the second occurrence has a genuinely different decision role, and no exact `fact_id`/`field_path` may appear three or more times. This is fact-level deduplication, not permission to make unsupported qualitative claims.
   Treat the six eligible KR foreign/institution 1/5/20-day values as one `canonical-supply-flow-tuple-v1` structured field set. Preserve every eligible canonical claim and its stable actor/horizon label; those repeated schema rows are not prose-diversity targets. Before naming an absorber, main buyer, or offsetting participant, apply `kr-investor-flow-reconciliation-v1`: `attribution_safe` must be true and the sentence must name `signal_basis_window`. When the basis is `mixed`, summarize the horizon tension and do not assign timeless absorption. `participant_taxonomy` distinguishes top-level participants from institution subclasses; never add an institution total to its subclasses or infer a missing participant from residual arithmetic. Analytical supply prose is optional and remains quality-checked: add it only for a verified accumulation, distribution, reversal, or horizon-divergence relationship, and omit it when it would merely repeat the tuple. When prose is used, make the actor the subject, complete the direction, quantity, and predicate, and do not mechanically reuse a `기록했고/기록했습니다` skeleton. Do not leave fragments such as `외국인 5일 순매수 X주는, 기관 5일 순매도 Y주를.` Check particles on Korean nouns and familiar metric labels, including natural forms such as `FCF를`, `투자 회수를`, `PBR을`, `ROE를`, `RR을`, and `HBM을`; never apply a global particle replacement.
   Keep financial-lineage cautions structured unless one limitation materially changes the user's interpretation. If visible, name the exact missing basis, such as amount period, consolidated/separate basis, share denominator, or security identity; do not prepend one portfolio-wide warning to otherwise company-specific prose. Likewise, `재고·CAPEX 이후 FCF·ROIC` is not a reusable stock conclusion. Tie cash conversion to a routed asset or mechanism such as memory inventory and HBM capacity, steel spread and working capital, or order-to-receivable conversion. When those Facts are absent, retain a company-specific Unknown or next check and suppress the generic tail.

6. Write one complete draft JSON document to the returned claim-specific `temp_output_path`. Set `claim_id` from the claim response and copy both Knowledge versions and checksums. Every `numeric_fact_refs[].ref_id` must be unique within its review, its placeholder must occur exactly once in its declared `text_ref`, and its `fact_id` must also be in `facts_used`. The binder converts the draft to the final schema-4 document. Do not write outside `data/ai_review`.
   A numeric placeholder represents the complete canonical phrase: source/instrument-aware label plus formatted value. Write `{{numeric:pe}}보다 {{numeric:fpe}}가 높습니다`, not `현재 PER {{numeric:pe}}보다 선행 PER {{numeric:fpe}}가 높습니다`. Prefer punctuation or separate clauses when joining placeholders; do not append an unverified `와/과` directly after a placeholder because the bound display may require the other postposition. The binder owns `현재 PER`, `시장 예상 fPER`, `내부 추정 fPER`, index names, market-series labels, night-futures product names, and zone endpoint roles. General context such as `TWD 기준인 {{numeric:revenue}}를 현재 증권 가격과 환산하지 않습니다` is allowed because it does not pre-author the numeric label or assert an unknown security type.

7. Validate and finalize it:

   ```bash
   .venv/bin/python -m app.jobs.ai_review validate --packet-id <packet-id> --claim-id <claim-id> --policy-version <analysis-policy-version>
   ```

   A nonzero result means the review was rejected. Do not weaken the validator or invent replacement facts. Correct the JSON from the same packet once, then validate again. When the five-day delivery pilot is enabled, a successful validator command invokes the backend-controlled single-delivery renderer; never send Telegram separately from the task.

## Output Rules

- Keep `schema_version`, `packet_id`, `analysis_policy_version`, `knowledge_version`, `knowledge_sha256`, `chart_knowledge_version`, `chart_knowledge_sha256`, `market`, and `assessment_date` identical to the packet, and use the active claim's `claim_id`.
- Produce exactly one stock review for every packet stock and no others.
- Separate concise facts used, interpretation, and unknowns. Do not output hidden reasoning or chain-of-thought.
- Explain why facts matter, what is noise, what expectations may already reflect, and what number or event should be checked next.
- Select no more than four decisive market changes. Distinguish index direction from breadth and sector concentration, and do not call a move broad risk-on when breadth is unavailable. Market context may be a tailwind or headwind, but it never changes a company thesis by itself.
- In market prose, state the verified market fact, its economic transmission, the relevant monitored portfolio group, and the next confirming metric. Do not repeat the deterministic market report or describe the review method.
- Keep FX levels separate from FX changes, yield levels separate from basis-point changes, and oil levels separate from oil returns. Rates, FX, and oil are generic market context unless a verified company exposure provides a transmission path. Never turn a rate increase into automatic growth-stock weakness, currency movement into automatic exporter benefit, or an oil move into a company earnings fact.
- Do not issue buy or sell orders.
- Keep the deterministic assessment as a guardrail. You may propose a different AI view in shadow, but never erase a deterministic warning.
- Never expose internal parser/provider fields. Never call a modeled estimate market or analyst consensus.
- If historical comparability is withheld, do not use a historical percentile or range.
- Apply the routed industry framework. A low peak-cycle PER alone is not a memory valuation conclusion; insurance does not use SaaS metrics; preliminary earnings do not prove FCF, inventory, ROIC, or balance-sheet changes.
- Use `chart_context` only when its quality permits. Interpret OHLCV Analyst values; never calculate RSI, MACD, Bollinger, support, resistance, ATR, Fibonacci, Elliott counts, target, stop, or risk/reward yourself.
- Treat `ohlcv-structure-v2` as the sole source for dynamic zones, boxes, Major Swings, tentative Elliott, Fibonacci, chart invalidation, risk/reward, and chart state. Local Pivot zones are not Major Swing anchors. Low-confidence Elliott and low-confidence Fibonacci are audit-only, while medium-confidence Fibonacci is context and never a sole core reason.
- Respect structure confidence and basis exactly: monthly support has no defined invalidation contract, so its invalidation and dependent risk/reward remain Unknown. Never skip a nearer monthly support to manufacture a farther daily/weekly invalidation.
- Keep stored thesis price rules separate from dynamic chart levels. A price threshold crossing is price confirmation, not an automatic business-thesis change. Once confirmation is crossed, discuss hold/retest, volume, positioning, and subsequent fundamental evidence.
- Treat `monitoring_state.current.price_structure` as the current price view and `registered_rule_state` as preserved thesis history. Never promote a crossed confirmation price to support without a verified overlapping dynamic support or retest. Prefer the nearest Strong/Medium dynamic support and resistance, and state their exact prices and current-price risk/reward when registered numeric facts permit it. If support, resistance, structural invalidation, or risk/reward is unavailable, say why and do not substitute a legacy registered support.
- Use `current_price_context` as the backend-selected common price input shared with deterministic fallback. A `HISTORICAL_REFERENCE` confirmation is past lifecycle context, not a future trigger; a `FUTURE_TRIGGER` remains secondary to available dynamic support, resistance, current-price risk/reward, and chart invalidation.
- Satisfy every `state_grounding_requirements.price` entry in `price_positioning`: cite its exact `fact_id` and submit numeric claims for each listed field path. For a zone, write independently grounded lower and upper prices. When `state_grounding_requirements.valuation` names `valuation:peer`, cite it and use at least two supported peer-relative numbers in `valuation_analysis`.
- Treat `monitoring_state.delta` as monitoring change, not thesis change. For confirmation lifecycle wording, `monitoring_state.delta.confirmation_transition` and `monitoring:confirmation_transition` are the only canonical transition source. Keep its previous state, current state, and direction aligned in every section. Price structure, supply, valuation percentile, and peer-relative valuation may move while `business_thesis_change` remains `no_material_change`.
- Comparative words such as improvement, deterioration, rise, fall, expansion, contraction, recovery, or slowdown require the exact canonical previous/current pair or registered delta for that same metric. A current RR alone supports only a current-state statement. Use `monitoring:risk_reward_transition` and both bound previous/current ratios for an RR change claim; do not infer direction from one value.
- Risk/reward basis is mandatory. `current_price_risk_reward_ratio` is the primary current-price judgment and binds as `현재가 기준 차트 손익비`. `support_entry_risk_reward_ratio` is a conditional scenario and binds as `동적 지지 접근 가정 차트 손익비`; use it only with explicit support-approach or support-zone conditional language. Never present the support-entry ratio as the current-price ratio or use a basis-free `차트 손익비` binding.
- For KR packets, treat 1-day supply as current momentum, 5-day supply as short-term positioning, and 20-day supply as medium-term positioning when the corresponding canonical facts exist. Explain horizon divergence without changing the fundamental status from flows alone. The displayed foreign/institution/individual tuple is `주요 3주체`, not proof that those actors exhaust the market. An absorber or leading-buyer claim requires full top-level participant reconciliation, `attribution_safe=true`, and the exact basis window; `mixed` or material omitted-participant flow requires qualified, non-exclusive language. Institution subclasses are diagnostic and must not be double-counted with institution total. For US packets, do not write 1-day/5-day/20-day investor horizons, daily/short/medium investor-flow prose, or foreign/institutional flow unless the packet explicitly supplies that US flow contract. Prefer stock-specific volume evidence or a concrete price/volume Unknown, and do not repeat one generic missing-flow sentence across the stock set.
- In valuation, separate current absolute multiples, the company's own historical percentile, and verified peer medians. A historical percentile means the current multiple is above that share of comparable past observations; never describe it as that percentage overvalued. Use peer figures only when `peer_valuation.available=true` and its metric sample is sufficient. Do not call a limited active-monitoring sample an industry-wide market average.
- Every valuation numeric placeholder needs its own draft-only `valuation_interpretation_refs` occurrence, including neutral absolute displays. Bind the occurrence to the narrow eligible interpretation fact for that field (`valuation:trailing_earnings`, `valuation:consensus_forward_earnings`, `valuation:current_pbr`, or the matching historical fact), and put that narrow fact in `facts_used` and `valuation_analysis.fact_ids`. A mixed aggregate `valuation:current` may remain the field-level numeric registry source, but it cannot own interpretation when `interpretation_eligible=false`. Supply `exact_text_span` for each occurrence; it may contain the same numeric placeholder as the prose. Use one of `absolute`, `historical`, `peer`, `market_expectation`, `trailing_forward_relation`, or `quality_unknown`, with the exact metric, fact, text reference, and comparison numeric references. One reference cannot approve a second sentence, metric, or interpretation in the same section. Historical high/low wording requires the matching visible percentile or comparison claim; peer premium/discount requires the matching peer metric and sample; market-expectation wording requires an independent backend expectation fact. A trailing/forward relationship may be interpreted only through `valuation:multiple_relation` when its price, security, share, currency, denominator, and forward-period status permit it. Do not say the forward period is unknown when that fact says `exact` or `provider_defined`, and do not interpret a relation when it says `unknown`. Two multiples cannot jointly be called burdensome unless each has its own comparison basis. A positive PBR cannot be interpreted together with negative BVPS on the same price, period, currency, and share basis. If those bases conflict or are unknown, withhold the dependent PBR and historical-PB interpretation while retaining independent safe facts. A neutral security/share/currency-basis caution also requires one `quality_unknown` occurrence owned by the exact `security_identity` or `security_basis` fact; internal words such as `denominator` or `per-share valuation` do not belong in Korean output.
- Chart state enums and risk/reward references are analytical context, not buy or sell commands. Chart Knowledge fair-value formulas never override backend valuation or Investment Knowledge v3 safety.
- Chart `INVALID` means the price scenario needs review; it never changes deterministic thesis invalidation. `HOLD` is holder context, not a new-entry signal, and `TRIM` means position-management pressure rather than a sell order. Risk/reward must use the engine's nearest eligible resistance and may not be improved by selecting a farther target.
- Treat `knowledge_routing.industry_routing.primary_framework` as company identity. When routing confidence is high, include it in `frameworks_used`; use only listed secondary frameworks for thematic or segment context. Thesis wording must not replace the primary industry framework.
- Use `company_profile` provenance and quality as an identity guardrail. `partial` or `ambiguous` profile data may reduce routing confidence; never infer a missing classification from the ticker, company name, or thesis theme.
- Resolve every draft `numeric_fact_refs[].text_ref` to its exact placeholder-bearing prose field. The backend-generated `numeric_claims[].text_ref` and `usage` remain fenced to that exact occurrence. One binding cannot cover the same number in another prose field, and a fact with the same numeric value cannot support a different semantic meaning. Never append a raw Korean particle directly to a numeric placeholder.
- Numeric semantics fail closed. An unregistered semantic or an entry with `prose_allowed=false` must remain absent from prose, even when its raw value exists in the packet.
- Every KR user-visible financial amount and directly attached growth or margin claim must use the binder-owned field label containing both the verified amount period and `연결 기준` or `별도 기준`. `IS`/`CIS`, a filing end date, or a plain statement title does not establish that basis. If the field-specific statement basis is `unknown` or `conflict`, omit the amount and its dependent growth interpretation.
- Write final price prose in particle-safe form such as `현재가는 {{numeric:price}}이며` or `{{numeric:price}} 수준의 현재 가격은`. Do not prepend `현재가` to the binder-owned `현재가 기준 차트 손익비` label. Telegram prose must not mention an engine, binder, validator, numeric registry, canonical semantic, or placeholder; describe the user-facing basis instead.
- Financial quality also fails closed. Treat `financial_quality.fields[*].state` values `denied` and `unknown`, plus numeric-registry rows with the same states, as audit-only: do not cite the ineligible fact as an earnings or valuation conclusion, do not restate its raw value, and do not infer qualitative strength from it. For valuation interpretation, cite the lineage-homogeneous facts (`valuation:trailing_earnings`, modeled or consensus forward earnings, `valuation:book`, modeled or consensus forward book, `valuation:historical_pe`, or `valuation:historical_pb`) supplied by the packet. When `valuation:current.interpretation_eligible=false`, it remains the numeric registry source only and cannot support qualitative prose. Explain a listed quality denial through the separate `financial_quality:*` fact without a number, then use only independent prose-eligible price, chart, supply, book-value, or verified-consensus facts.
- Security identity fails closed independently of financial quality. Use `security_identity:current` for whether the security itself is verified and `security_basis:current` for current-security denominator, share, and currency basis. Never describe a verified ADS/ADR identity as unverified merely because a valuation denominator is unavailable. Use security/share-basis-dependent valuation only when the associated registry rows are eligible. `conflict` and `unknown` are not verified non-depositary states: do not call the security ADR, ADS, common stock, or ordinary shares; use neutral phrases such as `현재 미국 상장 증권` or `현재 거래 증권`. Do not cite the denied multiple, restate its value, infer growth/cheapness/expensiveness from it, estimate an ADR ratio, or perform a currency conversion. A depositary ratio is verified only when value, direction, and authoritative provenance are all present. Explain the exact identity or valuation-basis gap through the matching fact, and continue with independent price, chart, volume, supply, or issuer-level financial facts that remain eligible.

## Runtime Boundary

Allowed writes:

- `data/ai_review/outbox/*.json.tmp`
- Files produced by the validator under `data/ai_review/outbox`, `history`, or `rejected`

Forbidden writes:

- `app/`, `tests/`, `ops/`, `.github/`, `.agents/`, `pyproject.toml`
- Database or Telegram state
