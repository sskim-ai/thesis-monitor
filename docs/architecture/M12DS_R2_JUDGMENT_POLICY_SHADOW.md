## Ownership

- The unchanged M12DR source authority answers whether an exact source may support an axis.
- A fresh typed Core selects observed propositions and separates confidence, configured conditions, and quality cautions.
- Directional propositions come only from already authorized frozen business facts. Truncated display text is resolved only against the exact original Fact projection and date, without changing either source.
- A comparative change is distinct from a positive or negative reported operating result. Revenue presence alone does not establish growth, profitability, persistence, or valuation.
- The backend materializes the selected proposition's factual text. Raw model interpretation is preserved separately; it cannot replace the canonical observation or add a fact.
- Configured conditions retain the source-owned logical expression and cannot become observed adverse events.
- Claim identity and effect binding are both verified against the current source-use manifest. Parent intersection is unchanged.

## Decision Axes

Overall supports BUY, SELL, and three HOLD reasons. Confidence and quality refs never enter the negative ref set. SELL requires observed adverse evidence. A positive-but-confidence-capped HOLD needs no fabricated negative claim.

Holder refs are mandatory for every stance. An explicit derived axis projection from eligible observed business claims permits REVIEW for active material risk, and REDUCE only for source-verified persistence or impairment. The current bounded observation adapter does not invent either. HOLDABLE cannot coexist with an active material holder trigger. This does not change raw-source permissions.

New Buyer is evaluated independently. ATTRACTIVE requires a valid fundamental discount; technical support is insufficient. AVOID requires observed material adverse risk without valid fundamental compensation. Holder REVIEW does not mechanically choose AVOID.

One directional buy score is model-owned; the backend owns the exact sell complement to ten. No label, score, or portfolio distribution quota is used.

## Ranges

Only existing canonical method candidates are consumed. Source-use, currency, security basis, and tier gates apply before selection. Every eligible method is retained for audit. Structural-cyclical methods that imply different directions relative to current price remain unresolved. Aligned methods use their intersection, never an average. One method remains low-confidence. No fitted tolerance is introduced.

Tactical watch zones have separate fields and remain available for WAIT/AVOID. They cannot create a fundamental range. All outputs include `TACTICAL_WATCH_ZONE != FAIR_VALUE`.

## Market Export
