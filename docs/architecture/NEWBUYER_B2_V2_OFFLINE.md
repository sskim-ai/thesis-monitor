# NewBuyer B2 v2 Offline Contract

REV56D adds `newbuyer-qualified-valuation-context-v2` as a separate,
default-OFF shadow builder. B2 v1, production callers, persistence, scheduling,
delivery, Core, Pass A, Overall, directional score and Holder are unchanged.
There is no model or provider dispatcher in this implementation.

## Authority and Eligibility

The caller pins the complete REV56C coverage and blocker-census receipt hashes.
The builder verifies the seals, exact security/generation binding, category
universe and recomputed census before resolving each relevant valuation metric.
A usable fact must have complete current category ownership. A typed denial
affects only its exact metric. A qualified fact and same-metric denial without
an explicit supersession contract are contradictory and reject the request.
Missing ownership is not a model-resolvable unknown.

`EVALUABLE` permits only SUPPORTIVE, NEUTRAL or BURDENSOME model judgments.
`ALL_RELEVANT_METRICS_UNUSABLE` requires an exact composite proof covering every
relevant metric and binds deterministic UNRESOLVED to that proof. Metric denial
never automatically becomes a global valuation or confidence veto. PBR without
the existing owned asset-relevance prerequisite remains NOT_RELEVANT.

## Business and Legacy Evidence

BUSINESS_GLOBAL source-quality denial changes business admissibility only when
its affected evidence references intersect the exact evidence references of
gate-supporting Core claims. A shared issuer, period or parent source is not
such a link. Unlinked denial remains CONTEXT_ONLY. Linked support is removed
only from the v2 admissibility view; independent support remains eligible and
the frozen Core is not edited. A failing v1 base gate cannot become a passing
gate by suppressing negative evidence.

Legacy confidence/quality claims retain their original bytes, effect and
materiality in backend audit. Their references and prose are excluded from the
provider request. They cannot create v2 valuation evidence or veto authority.
The reserved typed current-confidence interface is tested with fictional
owners; no such owner is manufactured for the frozen cohort.

## Separate Axes

Active material risk is AVOID-only. An ineligible business gate remains WAIT.
All relevant metrics unusable yields a proof-bound valuation WAIT when the
business gate otherwise passes. An explicitly authorized current confidence
veto is separate from metric and business denial.

For an eligible business gate without risk or authorized veto, SUPPORTIVE
valuation requires fundamental ATTRACTIVE, NEUTRAL requires valuation-neutral
WAIT and BURDENSOME requires valuation-burdensome WAIT. Backend timing is copied
unchanged. WAIT_FOR_ZONE or unresolved timing cannot erase fundamental
ATTRACTIVE. ATTRACTIVE is not a buy-now instruction; presentation metadata
prohibits a bare BUY label and grants no production delivery authority.

## Model Boundary

`provider_payload()` exports only prompt, whitelisted model input and projected
provider schema. Backend audit is not transmitted. Valuation and business
reference roles are disjoint; blocked values and legacy prose are absent.
The closed response schema fixes identity, timing, risk and allowed branch
predicates. It permits no free prose or new numerical facts. Source caveats
remain intact, including US FY1 product-policy authority and dated KIS research.
No implied EPS, ADR conversion or universal fair-value threshold is introduced.

The external trusted request SHA is required for payload and result validation.
Recomputing an envelope seal cannot change that expected identity. Exact
canonical request bytes are frozen only after local validation, and subsequent
changes require a new revision. REV57 execution is a separate task.
