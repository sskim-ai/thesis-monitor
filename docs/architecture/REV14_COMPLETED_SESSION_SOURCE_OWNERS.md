# REV14 Source Owners

Opt-in all-source proof only. No deployment, scheduler, delivery, or database
mutation. Existing investment reasoning and source-use policies are unchanged.

## Completed Bars

The immutable raw roles remain evidence. Before indicator calculation, the
completed-session owner selects daily rows through the calendar-verified target.
Partial weekly/monthly adjusted bars are derived from the exact daily sessions
through that target, never from the later query-time period candle. Existing
complete period bars retain their original source dates and values.

The component and replay consume identical eligible roles and bind their raw,
eligible, technical-input and fact-set hashes. No current-session row is hidden
after affecting an indicator. Invalid consumed daily rows cannot be aggregated
into an apparently valid partial bar. Unadjusted valuation history is never
derived from adjusted daily bars.

## KR Market

The opted-in collector separates current-session captures from completed-session
authority. When query date is later than target, exact ka20009 rows own the two
index values. Current-only sector, breadth and flow observations remain
unavailable. All source pages and their consumer-completeness proofs are retained.
Default production collector behavior is unchanged.

## News And Publication

Google query encoding has one serializer shared by descriptor and request.
Strict byte-level sealed-wire checking is unchanged. Pre-dispatch denial gets a
distinct terminal receipt and cannot become a no-event response or a missing-file
error. Naver uses instance-owned settings; sealed requests also bind the explicit
credential configuration digest, without exporting credential values.

ECOS KeyStatisticList exact registry rows use CYCLE, not query time, for daily,
monthly, quarterly or annual source periods. Fresh proof requires USD/KRW's daily
period to match the completed KR target. The old TIME-only synthetic fixture was
corrected to the provider's CYCLE shape; no production fallback was added.

Existing KOSPI200 night unavailability remains acceptable and renders the three
unavailable horizons. No stale substitute or additional product is admitted.
