## Fiscal Provenance

`FiscalComparabilityMetadata` admits four exact integer labels/counts only.
The audit registry recomputes issuer-declared annual comparability from the
policy receipt and both canonical occurrence dependencies. Unknown siblings,
missing relations, invalid types, or changed week counts fail closed. Registry
rows carry `audit_only=true`, no prose labels, no price/valuation/timing use,
and no independent directional authority. The generic numeric walker is unchanged.

## Filing Relationships

The exact SEC index and HTML references form `fpi-filing-document-graph-v1`.
A forwarding edge requires a 6-K primary cover, both index identities, an exact
financial/interim-report link, qualified fetched attachment, matching reporting
period, and unchanged artifact hashes. Conflicting attachments and unrelated
unresolved documents remain blockers. A cover never manufactures financial facts.
Graph nodes preserve original purpose and document hashes; the bound projection
retains forwarding provenance rather than deleting the cover.

## Bounded Document Slots

Index media metadata and exact HTML references separate image/support assets
from text document candidates. An HTML document cannot be excluded based on an
image-like filename/icon. All index identities and exclusions are retained.
There remain at most two attachment-content slots per filing, two boundary
candidates and one annual candidate, with fourteen total logical requests.
Excess plausible text documents deny selection. The reserved annual primary is
resolved independently of earlier accessions' attachment selection failures.
Every frozen slot is classified, including unused reserves. Slot plans and
terminal accounting are recomputed during network-free projection.

## Independent Fields

Revenue and operating income have separate completeness states. A qualified
comparison is preserved when another metric is absent, but partial-field stock
consumption requires a genuinely complete bounded source owner. Parser/context
uncertainty is never absence. Newer unresolved exact field context prevents an
older annual metric from serving as a current substitute. Existing inline/table
precedence, concepts, period normalization, valuation and quality policy remain.

## Offline Versus Current

REV15 raw fixtures are first authenticated using REV15's exact transport owner,
then passed to REV16 semantic owners as offline fixtures. This does not migrate
old receipts into current-source qualification. Live qualification must use a
new generation, newly frozen descriptors, fresh responses and two exact replays.
No code/config edits are allowed after that new generation starts.
# Annual Exhibit Routing

Annual-form links explicitly described by the official primary document as
articles of incorporation/association, officer certifications, consents, security
descriptions, land leases, or subsidiaries are recorded as non-financial request
exclusions. This routing classification does not grant content or field authority.
Financial statement/results labels override the exclusion; unknown descriptions
remain content candidates. The rule does not suppress unknown 6-K attachments.
The attachment cap remains two, and annual primary/index reserves are independent.
