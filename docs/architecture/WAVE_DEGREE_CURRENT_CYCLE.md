# Wave Degree and Current Cycle

## Contract

`wave-degree-current-cycle-v1` labels candidate scope as `GRAND_CYCLE`,
`PRIMARY_CURRENT_CYCLE`, `INTERMEDIATE`, or `TACTICAL`. This is a structural scope label, not a
claim that Elliott wave labeling is uniquely true.

Monthly candidate degree uses completed monthly span rather than a ticker or calendar-year table:

- `GRAND_CYCLE`: at least 84 monthly intervals.
- `PRIMARY_CURRENT_CYCLE`: 24 to 83 monthly intervals.
- `INTERMEDIATE`: fewer than 24 monthly intervals.
- `TACTICAL`: reserved for a future non-monthly candidate layer.

Each degree is ranked independently. Magnitude is capped as a soft component, while degree fit,
degree-appropriate recency, hard rules, Fib fit, pivot completion, and weekly confirmation remain
separate components. Grand-cycle history still contributes long-horizon SR context but cannot
hide a valid current-cycle candidate.

For SK hynix this deterministic split surfaces the 2023-01 W0 candidate in the current-cycle set
while retaining 2015/2016 candidates as grand-cycle context. No endpoint is hard-coded.
