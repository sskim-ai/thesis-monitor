## TSLA

The exact automotive group contains nine independent issuers after subject exclusion and SEC CIK
deduplication. Positive current TTM EPS leaves GM, THO and WGO for a PER median of 23.1763x. TSLA's
canonical current PER is 176.7188x, or 662.4979% above that median. The result is `MEDIUM`, not a
valuation verdict. Its message keeps the automotive margin, Robotaxi execution and FCF boundary.

The PBR sample has nine eligible issuers and a 1.3206x median, but it is not the selected display
metric because the existing TSLA message and industry interpretation are PER-centered. The PBR
distribution remains reproducible audit evidence.

## Result

US user-visible coverage is 1/13 overall and 1/8 among subjects where generic peer PER/PBR is
economically meaningful. Free forward estimates were not called: trailing coverage is below the
value gate, and estimate as-of/period/security-basis completeness would require a separate proof.
