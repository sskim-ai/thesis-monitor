# Phase 9.1A Comparable Period Audit

Primary comparison is prior-year same fiscal quarter or FY-versus-prior-FY. Q2 versus prior FY-end is rejected. Non-calendar issuers retain their fiscal quarter and actual balance dates. Current and prior facts require exact semantic, currency/unit, entity scope, and statement basis.

Revenue relation coverage: AR/revenue `14`, inventory/revenue `11`. COGS relation coverage: inventory/COGS `11`, AP/COGS `14`.

Revenue policy: `same filing, same fiscal period end/type, prior-year comparable; YTD preferred for Q2/Q3`.

COGS policy: `INCLUDE_SELECTIVELY_EXACT_SEMANTIC`. No DSO, DPO, inventory-days, or causal collection/demand/liquidity verdict is derived.
