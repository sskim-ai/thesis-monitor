## Scope

This audit reconstructs the immutable KR packet
`2026-08-25-kr-run-38-6cd8c5d5091b`. Provider recollection, production execution, Telegram
delivery, and database mutation were all zero.

## Symptom

The pre-repair market digest opened with valid domestic structure and flow, but its interpretation
returned to the generic portfolio macro sentence and its next check returned to US semiconductor
relative performance. Current KR data had already resolved the more decision-relevant local
question.

## Root Cause

The normalized market context was available, but the market renderer had no typed planning step
that carried evidence priority across judgment, interpretation, and next-check selection.
`important_changes` could surface local facts in the opening while the later blocks independently
selected generic text from the deterministic market summary.

This was not a provider, Kiwoom acquisition, date, numeric-binding, or semantic-ownership failure.
It was an evidence-prioritization gap after successful acquisition and normalization.

## Repair

`kr-market-digest-quality-v1` adds:

- deterministic `KR_DOMESTIC_CONTEXT_RICH` classification;
- typed P1-P5 evidence priority;
- one local-first plan spanning judgment, interpretation, and next check;
- explicit global-contradiction retention metadata;
- unchanged fail-closed concentration boundaries.

The rich predicate requires a completed/provider-complete final KR session, KOSPI and KOSDAQ
indices, reconciled scoped breadth for both markets, and at least one of market-wide flow,
size/style, or sector context. It is not a raw field-count test.

## Result

For the immutable packet, richness is `YES`. Judgment uses P1 market structure, interpretation uses
P2 market flow, and next check uses the unresolved P2 relation between KOSPI foreign selling and
broad positive participation. No global sentence is retained because no material contradiction is
needed. KOSPI concentration remains blocked and no concentration percentage is rendered.
