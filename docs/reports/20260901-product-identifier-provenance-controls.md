# Product-Identifier Provenance Controls

Canonical `KF-21`, `FA-50`, `F-35`, `B-21`, and `A320neo` mask only their owned spans. Adjacent `21`, `50`, `5`, and `12` remain numeric claims. Unproven `ZZ-999`, ranges, signed percentages, and signed currency remain under normal numeric validation. Dates and existing index-label controls remain intact.

`UNSUPPORTED_PRODUCT_IDENTIFIER_REJECTED = PASS`
