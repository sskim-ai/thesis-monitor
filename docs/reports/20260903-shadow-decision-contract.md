# Shadow Decision Contract

## Reasoning Order

`Fact -> business/earnings thesis -> market expectations -> valuation -> price/timing -> risks -> BUY drivers -> SELL drivers -> directional balance -> label -> new-buyer view -> holder view`

## Deterministic Label

- `BUY`: BUY balance >= 6.0
- `SELL`: SELL balance >= 6.0
- `HOLD`: otherwise
- Balance totals 10 and uses 0.5 increments. It is not a probability.

## Ownership

Business-thesis direction and price timing remain separate. A support break or unattractive entry does not by itself rewrite the business thesis. New-buyer and holder views are independent, and trim zones are review areas rather than mandatory sell targets.

## Guardrails

No fixed factor weights, invented stop loss, unsupported zone, fresh fact fetch, production mutation, or production delivery was permitted.
