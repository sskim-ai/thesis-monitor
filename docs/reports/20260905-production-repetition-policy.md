# Production Repetition Policy

Production classifies repeated spans as `BENIGN_TEMPLATE_REPEAT`, `REQUIRED_SAFETY_REPEAT`, `RENDERER_OWNED_REPEAT`, `MODEL_OWNED_SUBSTANTIVE_REPEAT`, or `MATERIAL_SPAM_REPEAT`.

Required safety and renderer-owned text never become model-quality vetoes. Short typed templates and structured numeric skeletons are soft quality. Long identical rationale repeated across at least three stocks with multiple evidence signatures remains a semantic hard block as material spam. This preserves user-facing protection while preventing harmless formatting regularity from suppressing an otherwise safe 15-message delivery.

The Run-57 second candidate becomes delivery-eligible after one deterministic language normalization pass: six rewrites, zero reference-invariant errors, zero binding errors, and three soft quality warnings. The material-spam fixture remains blocked.
