# Valuation Evidence Eligibility Contract

Date: 2026-09-05 KST

Status: shadow-only; no production mutation or delivery.

Each evidence ref now carries `prose_eligible`, `semantic_eligible`, `numeric_eligible`, `valuation_eligible`, and `valuation_role`. A valuation interpretation rejects every ineligible supporting ref. Security-basis evidence may own `CAUTION_ONLY`; it cannot launder an ineligible raw multiple into interpretation support.
